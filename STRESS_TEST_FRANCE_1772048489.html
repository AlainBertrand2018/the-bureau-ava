  5. Consider using a mixed-mode approach (e.g., online and paper-based) to reach a wider audience and accommodate different preferences.

  9. Utilize skip logic to tailor the survey experience and avoid irrelevant questions for individual respondents.
  10. Monitor response rates and follow up with non-respondents to improve data completeness.
