# AVA Scientific & Technical Foundations (Internal Protocol)

## 01. Objective
To establish a rigorous, non-negotiable scientific baseline for all research instruments and simulations generated or audited by AVA. This foundation ensures that SOB deliverables are not merely "AI-guessed" but "Scientifically Calibrated."

---

## 02. The Four Pillars of AVA Reliability

### Pillar I: Psychometric Validity (The Architect Protocol)
*   **Construct Alignment**: Ensuring every question maps accurately to the intended psychological or behavioral construct.
*   **Measurement Reliability**: Utilizing established survey-design frameworks (e.g., Likert scale optimization, avoiding acquiescence bias) to ensure consistent results.
*   **Linguistic Pragmatics**: Analyzing semantic ambiguity and syntax to ensure question interpretation is uniform across diverse demographic segments.

### Pillar II: Agent-Based Cognitive Simulation (Ghost Fieldwork)
*   **Cognitive Architecture**: Personas are built on established models of human memory and decision-making (Decision Theory).
*   **Stochastic Variance**: Implementing Monte Carlo methods to simulate real-world response noise and "Satisficing" behavior (respondent shortcuts).
*   **Demographic Calibration**: Quota-weighting based on National Census Data (Statistics Mauritius) and socio-economic stratification.

### Pillar III: Linguistic & Structural Diagnostics (The Audit Engine)
*   **Heuristic Evaluation**: Automated checking against "The Bureau's 8 Dimensions of Quality" (Neutrality, Clarity, Scope, etc.).
*   **Friction Analysis**: Measuring "Syntactic Complexity" to predict respondent dropout (Cognitive Load Theory).
*   **Bias Detection**: Identifying leading, loaded, and double-barrelled constructs using advanced NLP pattern-matching.

### Pillar IV: Data Integrity & Red-Teaming
*   **Adversarial Modeling**: Simulating malicious or professional survey-taking behavior to test the "Surface Hardness" of the instrument.
*   **Inconsistency Cross-Checking**: Mathematical verification that demographic answers align with behavioral responses within a 95% confidence interval.
