# AVA Tool Specification: The Architect Protocol (Genesis Suite)

## 01. Concept Overview
The **Architect Protocol** is AVA’s premier creation-tier tool. It solves the "Blank Page Problem" for Brand Managers and Researchers by generating a high-fidelity, 20-item research instrument from scratch. Unlike standard AI generation, this protocol includes a mandatory **Self-Correction Loop** where AVA audits her own design before final delivery.

---

## 02. The "Double-Blind" Workflow

### Phase A: Strategic Ingestion (The Brief)
Instead of a simple text box, the user enters a "Consultation Mode":
- **Objective**: What is the primary business or research goal?
- **Decision Matrix**: What action will be taken based on this data?
- **Audience DNA**: Who is being interviewed? (Demographics, Psychographics).
- **Tone & Style**: Formal, Conversational, Technical, or Brand-Bespoke.

### Phase B: Synthesis & Generation
AVA generates a structural skeleton:
- **Phase 1 (Warm-up)**: Low-friction questions to build rapport.
- **Phase 2 (Core KPIs)**: The "Gold Standard" questions targeting the primary objective.
- **Phase 3 (Diagnostic Deep-Dive)**: Probing for "Why" and "How."
- **Phase 4 (Demographic Anchor)**: Census-aligned classifiers.

### Phase C: The Self-Audit Loop (Internal Stress-Test)
AVA hands the questionnaire to her "Auditor" persona (The standard SOB Audit Engine):
- **Bias Check**: Does the phrasing lead the respondent?
- **Cognitive Load Check**: Is the flow too demanding?
- **Logical Flow**: Are skip-logics (if any) sound?
- **Rewrite**: AVA iterates on her own questions until they pass a >90/100 Quality Score.

### Phase D: The Final Instrument Package (The Hand-off)
The user receives a "Bureau-Certified" research package:
1. **The Instrument**: Ready-to-use 20-item questionnaire.
2. **The Predictive Outcome Report**: (Integrated with Ghost Fieldwork) A summary of expected data distributions.
3. **The Deployment Manual**: Best practices for when, where, and how to host the survey to minimize bias.

## 03. Technical Implementation

| Stage | Mechanism | Logic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ingestion** | Interactive Chat/Form | Extracting entity relationships and research constraints. |
| **Generation** | Multi-persona Prompting | Using a "Research Lead" persona to draft content. |
| **Validation** | SOB Audit Engine | Measuring structural quality dimensions (Neutrality, Clarity, etc.). |
| **Refinement** | Recursive Rewriting | Self-correcting until quality thresholds are met. |
