# Operations & Marketing Strategy

## 1. Operational Model (The "Lean Bureau")
* **Staffing:** Solopreneur (You).
    * *Role:* "Principal Consultant" (You sell the expertise, the AI does the labor).
* **Infrastructure:**
    * **Frontend:** Vercel (Next.js hosting) - Free/Pro Tier.
    * **Backend:** Local Python Environment (initially) -> Google Cloud Run (later).
    * **Database:** Local JSON/CSV (initially) -> Google BigQuery (later).
* **Cost Control:**
    * Fixed Costs: ~$0 (Hosting free, Laptop owned).
    * Variable Costs: ~$0.50 per Survey Run (API Tokens).
    * *Implication:* You are profitable from Client #1.

## 2. Marketing Strategy: "The Insurance Pitch"
**Core Message:** "Market Research is expensive. Bad Market Research is fatal. For Rs 5,000, we insure your strategy."

### Channel A: The Direct "Agency" Partner (B2B)
* **Target:** Local Marketing Agencies / Advertising Firms.
* **Pain Point:** They fear presenting bad data to big clients.
* **The Pitch:** "White-label my service. You sell the 'AI Audit' to your client for Rs 10,000. I do it for Rs 5,000. You keep the margin and look like a genius."

### Channel B: The Academic Angle
* **Target:** University of Mauritius / UTM PhD Students & Researchers.
* **Pain Point:** Thesis rejected due to "poor sampling methodology."
* **The Pitch:** "Validate your questionnaire methodology with a synthetic pilot study before you go into the field."

### Channel C: LinkedIn "Public Build"
* **Tactic:** The "Tear Down."
* **Action:** Take a public, controversial survey (e.g., a recent political poll or a brand failure). Run it through your simulator. Publish the results: *"Our AI Agents predicted this product would fail 2 weeks ago. Here is the chart."*
* **Goal:** Establish Thought Leadership without spending ad money.

## 3. The Sales Cycle
1.  **Inbound:** Client drops file on Landing Page.
2.  **Automated Hook:** They get the "Free Instant Audit" email (Automated).
3.  **The Close:** You email: "The full simulation shows a 40% rejection rate on your pricing. Want to see the full data? Pay here."
