## ◈ Overview
The Survey Optimization Bureau (SOB) has moved beyond "AI Generation" into **Autonomous Methodology Orchestration**. This knowledge base defines the core technical and strategic protocols that differentiate the Bureau from generic LLM-based tools.

---

## ◉ Core Architectural Units

### 1. AVA Architect V2.0 (The Logic Core)
The central intelligence unit responsible for multi-phased mission design. Unlike chatbots, AVA constructs a full **Methodological Framework** first, ensuring that every research instrument is grounded in institutional logic before a single question is drafted.

### 2. Sentinel (Live Grounding Unit)
The Sentinel unit performs real-time deep-scans of economic, cultural, and fiscal landscapes. It ensures that the Bureau's "Genesis Suite" is grounded in current reality (e.g., precise inflation rates, salary benchmarks, and cultural taboos) rather than legacy training data.

### 3. Adjudicator (Adversarial Stress-Testing)
The Adjudicator runs **n=5 Dynamic Synthetic Archetypes** through every instrument. It identifies:
- **Cognitive Friction:** Where a respondent might be confused.
- **Cultural Taboos:** Where a question violates local norms.
- **Psychometric Drift:** Where the question fails to measure the intended KPI.

## ◉ The Sharpening & Futureproofing Protocols

### ◈ The Sharpening Unit (`_sharpen_text`)
A proprietary semantic filter that acts as a gatekeeper between AI output and the Human render.
- **Meta-Talk Expunge:** Automatically strips conversational AI filler (e.g., "Considering the difficulties...", "Example: ...").
- **Deduplication Logic:** Normalizes and removes redundant sentences across the entire dossier.
- **Direct Command Enforcement:** Ensures that the research instrument contains only actionable questions, not rumination.

### ◈ Futureproof Rendering
- **Sticky Institutional Nav:** A persistent, blurred glass navigation bar for long-form report accessibility.
- **Smooth-Scroll Architecture:** Enhances the "institutional feel" by providing seamless transitions between technical sections.
- **Active Anchor Mapping:** Every legal, technical, and scientific section is uniquely ID'd for cross-referencing and print-to-PDF reliability.

## ◉ Institutional Standards
1. **Zero-PII Architecture:** No personally identifiable information ever enters the Bureau's processing layer.
2. **Methodological Integrity:** A minimum score of 95/100 is required for Mission Certification.
3. **The "Human Feel":** Technical documentation must be high-density but semantically clear, avoiding the "chatty" nature of legacy AI systems.
